Grade Predictor

A small regression model that estimates a likely final exam score from a student's earlier assignment grades.

StackPython, pandas, scikit-learn RoleSolo, weekend project StatusFinished, exploratory

What it does

Given a student's grades on the first few assignments of a course, the model predicts a likely range for their final exam score, so they can gauge early whether they're on track.

How it's built

Built on an anonymised grade dataset from a past course. Data cleaning and feature engineering happen in pandas; the model itself is a simple ridge regression from scikit-learn, chosen after comparing it against a couple of heavier alternatives that didn't perform meaningfully better on this dataset size.

What I learned

  • Why a simpler model can beat a complex one on small data
  • Basic feature engineering and train/test splitting discipline
  • Communicating a prediction as a range, not a false-precise number
