3D Fiber Detection & Analysis

A computer vision pipeline for detecting and tracking individual fibers in 3D image data and analyzing their orientation and curvature.

StackPython, NumPy, OpenCV, scikit-image RoleResearch project StatusIn progress

What it does

The project aims to detect individual fibers in 2D image slices and track them across the z-direction to reconstruct the fibers as 3D curves. The reconstructed fibers can then be analyzed based on their geometric properties.

How it's built

The image data consists of sequential 2D TIFF slices. Fibers are first detected within individual slices and then tracked between consecutive slices to form continuous 3D fiber trajectories. From these trajectories, fiber orientation is described using azimuth and elevation angles, while the geometry of the curves is used to estimate their curvature.

Analysis

The resulting fiber properties are analyzed using distributions and histograms of orientation angles and curvature. The method is also evaluated on downscaled versions of the original data to investigate how image resolution affects fiber detection and tracking performance.

Reference
