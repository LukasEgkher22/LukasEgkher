DTU RoboCup 2025

An autonomous robot developed for the DTU RoboCup 2025, combining navigation, sensing, mechanical design, and custom 3D-printed mechanisms to complete a challenging multi-stage obstacle course.

RobotNewton RoleRobotics & mechanical development Result2nd place

The Challenge

DTU RoboCup is an autonomous robotics competition where teams develop a robot capable of navigating a complex track and completing a series of challenges within a limited amount of time. The 2025 track included ramps, stairs, a seesaw, gates, moving obstacles, a golf-ball challenge, luggage delivery, and ball sorting.

Our Robot

We started with the standard robot platform provided for the competition. The robot was equipped with distance sensors at the front, two independently controlled wheels, and a front-mounted mechanism that could be moved vertically.

My Contribution

I focused heavily on extending the robot's physical capabilities. I designed and 3D-printed custom mechanical components that allowed the robot to interact with objects on the track, including picking up and transporting balls and moving luggage.

Developing these mechanisms required extensive testing and iteration. Many of the solutions only worked under very specific conditions, so a large part of the project consisted of repeatedly testing, identifying failure modes, and redesigning the mechanisms until they became reliable enough for competition.

Engineering Challenges

  • Designing custom 3D-printed mechanisms within the constraints of the existing robot
  • Developing reliable mechanisms for picking up and transporting objects
  • Handling uneven surfaces, ramps, stairs, and the seesaw
  • Using distance sensors for reliable navigation and obstacle detection
  • Testing and iterating through a large number of real-world failure cases
  • Balancing reliability and the number of challenges attempted within the time limit

Result

After extensive development and testing, our robot Newton achieved 2nd place in the DTU RoboCup 2025. The report can be opened and read below.
